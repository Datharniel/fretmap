# Fretmap

**Chords, scales and arpeggios for guitar and bass, in any tuning.**

👉 **Open the app: [datharniel.github.io/fretmap](https://datharniel.github.io/fretmap/)**

Free, runs in the browser, works on phone and desktop. No account, no install.

## What it does

- **Chords**: pick a root and a chord type and see every playable shape in your tuning, with finger numbers, barres and inversions. Tap a shape to hear it.
- **Scales**: 17 scales, from pentatonics and the modes to harmonic minor, Phrygian dominant and double harmonic. See them on the whole neck, part of the neck, a single string, CAGED boxes or 3 notes per string.
- **Arpeggios**: chord tones across the neck, with the scales that fit over each chord.
- **Name a chord**: tap notes on the fretboard and Fretmap tells you what chord you are holding.
- **Practice**: metronome with subdivisions, tap tempo and a speed trainer. It can light up the scale one note per click, so you can play along.
- **Tuner**: uses your microphone and follows the strings of the tuning you picked, including drop tunings.

## Instruments and tunings

6, 7, 8 and 9-string guitar and 4, 5 and 6-string bass, with standard, drop and open tunings. Every tuning shows its notes string by string, and you can edit any string and save your own tunings.

Also included: note names or intervals, sharps or flats, and a left-handed view.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. The tuner listens to your microphone only while it is on, and nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. Settings and saved tunings are stored in your own browser.
